Privacy Policy for "Seoul Office: Intern to CEO"

iamguno ("the Developer") respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game "Seoul Office: Intern to CEO"
(Korean title: 인턴 키우기, "the Game") and complies with applicable privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea.

1. Information We Collect

The Developer does not directly collect any personal information.

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • The Game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

However, the Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, and Google may process the following information to do so:

  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID), device information (model, OS version, language), IP address, and ad impression and click records
  • Purposes: showing ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud

Google's processing of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and Google's advertising policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

To let you continue your progress, the Game stores the following information only on your device.
This information is never sent to the Developer or anyone else, and it cannot be used to identify you (this is separate from the ad information in Section 1).

  • Game progress (stages, rank, stats, items, currencies, attendance records, etc.)
  • Sound, music and language settings
  • The time you last closed the Game (used to calculate rewards earned while you were away)

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the Developer does not collect personal information, it does not share it with or entrust its processing to any third party.

To show ads, the information in Section 1 is transferred to and processed by Google LLC (United States).

  • Recipient: Google LLC (United States)
  • Items transferred: advertising ID, device information, IP address, ad impression and click records
  • When and how: over the internet, when an ad is loaded or shown in the Game
  • Retention: according to Google's policies

4. Retention and Deletion

The Developer does not keep any personal information. Game data on your device is deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
Ad information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

5. Your Rights

You can delete all information stored by the Game at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.
You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
For any privacy questions, please contact us using the details below.

6. Children

The Developer does not directly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Ad-related processing is described in Section 1.
